# Two-stage novelty and open-status gates

N1 before research promotion: freeze exact quantified statement and equivalent forms, search terms, original references, forward citation trail, current 2025–2026 status, nearest 3–5 theorems with theorem number and exact scope, counterexamples, common consequences, semantic comparator, practical value. Distinguish full text/proof from abstract and search excerpt. Source no-hit never confirms open. N2 after actual proof: repeat against exact achieved theorem and newest literature; independent mathematical scrutiny required. Open labels: CONFIRMED_OPEN_FROM_CURRENT_AUTHORITATIVE_EVIDENCE, STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN, STATUS_UNCERTAIN, ALREADY_SOLVED, MATERIALLY_SUPERSEDED. Mathematical novelty verdict distinct: CANDIDATE_DISTINCT, NOVELTY_UNCERTAIN, NOT_NOVEL, ALREADY_PROVED, REFUTED. Prior results are to be reused not disguised as new proofs. Proven dependency versus method transfer versus parallel versus speculative must be explicit.
