# Mathematical charter

Pursue an unconditional proof of the Erdős–Rado sunflower conjecture. For each fixed integer r≥3, seek C_r independent of k and universe size such that every k-uniform set family larger than C_r^k contains r distinct sets whose pairwise intersections coincide. Maintain a sequence of independently significant, source-checked results in delta-system extraction, robust sunflowers, intersection structure and constructive algorithms. Existing full conjecture open per Rao (2026), while ALWZ established approximately (C_r log k)^k bounds and 2026 results cover restricted settings. Do not conflate independent proofs, log-gap narrowing or special cases with final solution. The first target is the internal-kernel issue explicitly posed by Janzer–Jin–Sudakov–Wu (2026 §6), subject to new full-source novelty review. Quantum shadow tomography and set-system Ramsey applications are *published links to related methods*, not demonstrated outputs from our future theorem.
