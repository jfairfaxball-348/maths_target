1. **First paper:** Resolve or improve the internal-witness quantitative delta-system extraction question of Janzer et al. (2026 §6). **Link to next:** PLAUSIBLE_METHOD_TRANSFER.
2. **Structure:** Prove sharp extraction/obstruction structure for fixed k or structured hypergraphs. **Link to next:** PARALLEL_PUBLICATION_OPPORTUNITY.
3. **Infinite/unifying:** Obtain single-exponential quantitative internal-kernel results for an infinite parameter family. **Link to next:** PLAUSIBLE_METHOD_TRANSFER.
4. **Major intermediate:** Develop new robust sunflower extraction that removes a major log(k) loss for broad families. **Link to next:** SPECULATIVE_CONNECTION.
5. **Ultimate:** Prove the unrestricted Erdős–Rado sunflower conjecture. **Link to next:** none — final.
**Prior art and risk:** The 2026 question may be almost as hard as the target; k=3 must be separately audited.
