### SUN
STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN: For each r≥3, ∃C_r so every k-uniform set family of size >C_r^k contains an r-sunflower.
Closest currently inspected comparator(s): Janzer–Jin–Sudakov–Wu (2026), Theorem 1.7 and Section 6; Rao, The story of sunflowers, JLMS 113 (2026), doi:10.1112/jlms.70380; Ge–Wang–Xu–Zhao, Bounded VC-dimension implies the Erdős–Rado sunflower conjecture. Entry gap: For all k,m≥2, every k-uniform family F contains F' with |F'|≥c_k m^(-k)|F| and each intersection of two distinct F' members is a kernel of an m-petal sunflower wholly within F'.
Novelty / proof risk: The 2026 question may be almost as hard as the target; k=3 must be separately audited.. Current frontier confidence: directly source-posed Janzer §6, but no 2026 forward-citation exhaustion.
