# Exactly ten candidates and frozen first targets

## SUN — Erdős–Rado Sunflower Conjecture

**Field:** extremal set theory. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** For each r≥3, ∃C_r so every k-uniform set family of size >C_r^k contains an r-sunflower.

**First proposed target:** For all k,m≥2, every k-uniform family F contains F' with |F'|≥c_k m^(-k)|F| and each intersection of two distinct F' members is a kernel of an m-petal sunflower wholly within F'.

**Evidence:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00493-026-00222-1; https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70380; https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18995. **Main obstacle:** The 2026 question may be almost as hard as the target; k=3 must be separately audited.

**Application evidence:** B: restricted-intersection bounds and quantum shadow tomography via published delta-system tools; not deployment-ready.

**Status caveat:** Proposed first targets are NOT certified original opportunities; source-level N1 required before mathematical investigation. 

## ROT — Rota's Basis Conjecture

**Field:** matroids. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** Every n labeled rank-n matroid bases can be rearranged into n disjoint transversal bases.

**First proposed target:** Unrestricted rank-five transversal basis decomposition for every rank-five matroid, with labeled occurrences.

**Evidence:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05601. **Main obstacle:** Rank-five open-status not directly established; earlier formalisation competition exists.

**Application evidence:** C: constructive base packing may inform matroid algorithms and scheduling, not a direct consequence of existence.

**Status caveat:** Proposed first targets are NOT certified original opportunities; source-level N1 required before mathematical investigation. 

## MDS — MDS Conjecture for Linear Codes

**Field:** coding theory. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** For nontrivial linear [N,k] MDS codes over F_q, N≤q+1 except the standard even-q k=3 or dual exceptions with N≤q+2.

**First proposed target:** Find a new provable residual dimension range for composite q beyond the bounds claimed by Fan (2026), after auditing that preprint.

**Evidence:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03740; https://arxiv.org/html/1903.05265v1. **Main obstacle:** 2026 dramatic dimension-range preprint must be checked; algebraic competition intense.

**Application evidence:** A: MDS codes support error correction and distributed storage; longer hypothetical parameters do not directly improve deployed codes.

**Status caveat:** Proposed first targets are NOT certified original opportunities; source-level N1 required before mathematical investigation.

## UNC — Frankl's Union-Closed Sets Conjecture

**Field:** extremal set theory / information theory. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** Every nontrivial finite union-closed family has an element occurring in at least half of its members.

**First proposed target:** Prove a new 1/2-frequency result for a structurally defined non-subsumed family of Horn Boolean systems.

**Evidence:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02295; https://doi.org/10.1007/s11083-025-09717-w. **Main obstacle:** Exact Horn subclass has not been frozen and may be subsumed; latest October 2026 entropy threshold is moving.

**Application evidence:** B: Horn Boolean functions and entropy inequalities; specific operational gains speculative.

**Status caveat:** Proposed first targets are NOT certified original opportunities; source-level N1 required before mathematical investigation. 

## CH — Caccetta–Häggkvist Directed Girth Conjecture

**Field:** directed graphs. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** A simple n-vertex digraph of minimum outdegree ≥r has directed girth at most ceil(n/r).

**Evidence:** https://dwest.web.illinois.edu/openp/cacchagg.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02902. **Main obstacle:** 2019 result not a current status audit; much recent graph activity unexamined.

**Application evidence:** C: directed network feedback-cycle guarantees, no certified engineering performance.

## TWIN — Twin Prime Conjecture

**Field:** analytic number theory. **Open status:** STRONG_EVIDENCE_OPEN. **Headline:** There are infinitely many primes p with p+2 also prime.

**Evidence:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02851; https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14810. **Main obstacle:** Sieve parity obstruction and tiny accessible proof window; weak direct practical value.
